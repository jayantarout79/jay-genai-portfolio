# KPI Definitions

## Purpose
This document lists commonly used KPIs and their general definitions.

## Example KPIs
- Headcount: Number of active employees at a given point in time
- Attrition Rate: Percentage of employees who exit during a defined period
- Absence Rate: Ratio of absent work hours to scheduled hours

## Notes
- KPI calculations may vary slightly by reporting context
- Business stakeholders should validate KPI interpretations before use
- Not all KPIs are suitable for all dashboards

## Governance
Changes to KPI logic should follow the schema change and review process.
