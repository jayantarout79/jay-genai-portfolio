# AI Assistant Usage Policy

## Purpose
Define acceptable usage of internal AI-powered assistants.

## Allowed Use
- Querying approved datasets
- Assisting with data discovery
- Supporting operational troubleshooting

## Restrictions
- AI assistants should not be used as the sole source of truth
- Sensitive or personal data must not be exposed
- Generated outputs must be validated before decision-making

## Limitations
AI assistants rely on available documentation and metadata and may not always provide complete answers.
