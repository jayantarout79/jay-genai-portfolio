# Data Quality Policy

## Objective
Ensure data used for reporting and analytics meets acceptable quality standards.

## Key Dimensions
- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Timeliness
- Consistency

## Quality Checks
- Null value thresholds
- Referential integrity checks
- Volume anomaly detection
- Schema validation

## Responsibilities
Data engineers are responsible for implementing quality checks.
Business teams are responsible for validating business meaning.

## Exceptions
Some datasets may temporarily bypass quality thresholds during peak events or known upstream issues.
